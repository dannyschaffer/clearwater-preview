Our off-grid package takes you from a dry patch of garden to running borehole water in the house. We survey the property to find where to drill, drill the hole, case it, and fit the pump, tank and filters. One team does the whole job, so there's no chasing three different contractors.

Municipal water in Joburg goes off more often every year and costs more every year. With a borehole, you have your own supply and stop paying for most of the water you use.

Why customers choose it

  • One all-inclusive price, quoted upfront
  • Water during municipal outages and shutdowns
  • Lower monthly water bills
  • Filtered, UV-treated water for the whole house

Questions about borehole drilling & off-grid packages

How much does a borehole cost?

Our complete off-grid package starts at R59 000. That covers the survey, drilling up to 60 m, casing, pumps, tank, filtration and UV. If your site needs a deeper hole or a bigger system, we quote it upfront before any work starts.

How deep will you drill?

The package includes drilling up to 60 m. The survey tells us where the water-bearing ground is, and if your property needs more depth we tell you and quote it before we drill.

Can I add a softener or a VSD pump?

Yes. We fit water softeners, ozone generators and variable speed drive (VSD) pumps on top of the standard package. VSD pumps keep pressure steady and use less power.
