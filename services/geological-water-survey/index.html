Drilling in the wrong spot is the most expensive mistake you can make with a borehole. A survey tells you where the water-bearing ground is before the rig arrives, so you drill once.

Our survey is included in the off-grid package. You can also book it on its own if you want answers before you commit.

Why customers choose it

  • Higher chance of a productive borehole
  • Avoid dry holes and unstable ground
  • Know the costs before you drill
  • Better water quality from the right source

Questions about geological water survey & borehole siting

Is the survey included if I take the drilling package?

Yes. The geological survey and siting are part of the off-grid package. On its own, the survey costs R3 500.

What do I get at the end?

A written report with the recommended drilling spot and what to expect from it.

Do I need a survey for a small garden borehole?

We recommend one for every borehole. It's a small cost next to drilling a hole in the wrong place.

What's included

  • On-site survey
  • Hydrogeological assessment
  • Geophysical survey of water-bearing formations
  • Borehole siting
  • Written report and drilling recommendations
